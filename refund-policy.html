Project agreement

Each project's scope, fees, milestones and payment schedule should be agreed in a written proposal or agreement before work begins. The specific signed agreement and applicable requirements take precedence.

Before work begins

To cancel a project, submit a written request. If work has not begun, payments will be reviewed; previously agreed and incurred non-recoverable costs may be deducted.

After work begins

Refunds will be reviewed based on completed work, committed resources and agreed milestones. Fees for delivered or approved work are generally non-refundable, subject to applicable requirements and the written agreement.

Third-party costs

Domain, hosting, license and other third-party charges are subject to the providers' refund terms. These costs should be discussed in advance as part of the project.

Quality concerns

If delivery does not match the agreed scope, submit your concerns in writing. We will first address corrections or an agreed remedy; any eligible refund will be assessed under the project agreement.

How to request a refund

Send the project name, invoice reference, payment details and reason for cancellation or refund to our contact email. The review outcome and any applicable refund timing will be provided in writing.
