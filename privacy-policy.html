About this policy

This policy explains how SoftEdge Systems LLC uses your information in connection with its website and consulting services.

Information we may collect

When you contact us by email, phone or a project inquiry, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, business details and project information you choose to share. Do not include passwords or private credentials in an inquiry.

How we use information

We use your details to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, provide agreed services and communicate about projects. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Forms and cookies

This static website's form does not store information on a website server; it opens a draft in your email application. This package does not include analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. The hosting provider may maintain operational logs.

Sharing and security

Relevant information may be shared with providers needed for service delivery or when required under applicable requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but cannot guarantee absolute security of internet transmissions.

Retention and your choices

Information may be retained as needed for project delivery, communication and applicable obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion through our contact email, subject to relevant obligations.

Updates and contact
