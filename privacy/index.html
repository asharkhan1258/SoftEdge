SoftEdge Systems LLC is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website (the “Website”), how we use it, how we protect it, and the rights you have regarding your personal information.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not access or use our Website.

1. What Personal Data We Collect

  • Contact Information – Name, email address, telephone number, company, and mailing address.
  • Business Information – Information about your organization, software requirements, and services requested.
  • Account Data – Information tied to registered user accounts, if applicable to an agreed service. This website does not currently provide account registration.
  • Location & Technical Information – Hosting or service providers may process IP addresses, browser type, device information, operating system, and browsing activity.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies – Site usage, analytics, and preference data where these technologies are enabled.
  • Communications – Records of inquiries, support requests, and feedback.
  • Email Interconnectivity – Email-open and link-click information where an email service with these features is used.
  • Media Submissions – Images or media you share during a project, including embedded metadata.
  • Sensitive Information – Only information you expressly provide and that is relevant to the requested service. Do not send passwords, private credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and manage the software development and consulting services you request.
  • Manage project requests and administer service accounts, if applicable.
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests.
  • Analyze site usage and improve functionality where analytics are enabled.
  • Send relevant updates, newsletters, and marketing, with consent where required.
  • Personalize project communications and relevant content.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or misuse.
  • Comply with applicable obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to operate the Website and analyze usage, including essential, functional, and analytics cookies, where enabled. Third-party integrations, such as embedded video or social media, may use their own cookies.

This website package currently includes no analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party embeds. Its contact form opens an email draft and does not submit information to a website database.

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We may share data only with service providers, affiliates where relevant, or as required by law. We do not sell your information.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for business or legal purposes. Where applicable, analytics information is typically retained for up to one year and contact inquiries for up to six months, unless ongoing service delivery or applicable obligations require longer retention.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us to make a request.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and service, including restricted access and secure transmission where available. The deployed Website should use HTTPS. No method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

In the event of a breach, we will notify affected users as required.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Website and services are not directed toward children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
