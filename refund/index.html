Your Rights Regarding Refunds

In the event of a refund or cancellation, both SoftEdge Systems LLC (the “Consultant”) and the Client agree to the following, subject to applicable requirements:

Refund and Cancellation

Before the work begins: A full refund of any prepaid amount shall be issued to the Client.

After the work begins but before the service is fulfilled: A partial refund of the prepaid amount shall be issued based on the portion of work completed. The completed work and refund calculation will be explained to the Client.

After the service is fulfilled: No refund shall be issued to the Client, except where required by applicable law.

Consultant-Initiated Cancellation

If SoftEdge Systems LLC cancels the service for any reason before fulfillment, a full refund shall be issued to the Client. The refund shall be credited to the original payment method within 30 days of service cancellation.

After fulfillment of the service, the Client may not be eligible for a refund and remains responsible for agreed fees for completed services, subject to applicable requirements.

Returns and Payment Disputes

Our services involve programming, consulting, and digital deliverables rather than physical products, so physical returns do not apply. For a billing concern or disputed charge, please contact us with the project and transaction details so we can review it. This does not restrict any rights you may have with your payment provider or under applicable law.
